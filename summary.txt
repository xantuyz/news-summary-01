World news, Wednesday 11 November 2026:
1. Russia and Ukraine ended Abu Dhabi talks with their largest prisoner swap yet, but no ceasefire; envoy Jared Kushner said trust was lacking (Reuters, AP).
2. World leaders opened COP31 in Antalya, Turkey, with 77 leaders due to speak; the US sent none (AFP, UNFCCC).
3. The US charged three Iran-linked men over nearly 20 alleged attacks in Europe and the US, including a foiled RAF Fairford plot; one allegedly directed it from a New York jail (BBC, US Justice Dept).
4. The US House voted 211-204 to send impeachment articles against Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth to committee; sponsor Thomas Massie was the only Republican opposed (Reuters).
5. Democrat Scott Colom beat Republican Cindy Hyde-Smith in Mississippi, the state's first Black senator since 1874; Democrats lead 55-44 in the Senate, Alaska uncalled (Reuters).
6. Congo's Ebola outbreak passed 11,000 cases and 5,387 deaths — the country's deadliest, caused by Bundibugyo virus, with no vaccine (AP).
7. Kenya discharged its last Ebola patient, starting a 42-day countdown to Ebola-free status; it had nine cases, two deaths (Reuters, WHO).
8. El Salvador said its troops and Haitian police destroyed Haiti's 400 Mawozo gang headquarters; reports of about 20 killed are unconfirmed (BBC).
9. US prices rose 0.4% in October, hotter than expected, putting the Fed on course for a December rise; gold fell 2.7% to $4,031 an ounce (BLS, CNBC).
10. A Florida judge let Donald Trump's $10bn BBC defamation case over an edited Panorama clip proceed, dropping a trade-practices claim (Reuters).
11. China's Wang Yi called his talks with US Secretary of State Marco Rubio "positive and constructive" ahead of a possible Trump-Xi meeting (Reuters, Xinhua).
12. A bus hit 32 vehicles in Seongnam, South Korea, seriously injuring nine, injuring 48 more; the driver blamed failed brakes (Reuters, Yonhap).
13. Asian shares fell on the hot US inflation data, with the Nikkei and Kospi down (NDTV Profit).
